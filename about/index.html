About Michigan Courts

One court of justice for Flint and Genesee County: how the courts are organized and where to find what you need.

Our mission

Michigan's courts resolve disputes fairly, apply the law the same way to everyone, and keep their work open to the public. This website is the single place to look up cases, file documents, pay fines, read the rules, and find the court that handles your matter.

The portal is maintained by the State Court Administrative Office (SCAO), which supports the courts with shared services and standard forms.

How the courts are organized

Michigan's court system has two levels. Cases begin in the trial court and, when a party appeals, can be reviewed by the Supreme Court.

Genesee County District Court

Flint, Michigan

The trial court for Genesee County. It hears criminal, civil, traffic, and felony cases, and it's where most people first come into contact with the courts.

About the District Court

Michigan Supreme Court

Lansing, Michigan

The court of last resort. It reviews appeals from the trial court and has the final word on Michigan law.

About the Supreme Court

Several offices and agencies support the Supreme Court and oversee the legal profession and the judiciary. You can find them under Court Offices & Agencies in the Courts menu.

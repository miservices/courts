Proposed Rule Amendments & Adoptions

The Michigan Supreme Court publishes proposed amendments and adoptions to the Michigan Court Rules, Rules of Evidence, Michigan Rules of Professional Conduct, Rules Concerning the State Bar, Michigan Code of Judicial Conduct, and other administrative rules. Public comment is invited on all open proposals before the Court takes final action.

About This Page

Each proposal below includes an ADM docket number, the action being taken (amendment or adoption), a plain-language summary, and a link to the full proposal document. Proposals are listed from newest to oldest. Once the comment period for a proposal has closed, the record is preserved here for reference.

After the comment period closes, the Court reviews all timely-submitted comments before issuing a final order. The Court is legally required to consider all comments before formally deciding any proposal. Final orders are published separately in the Court's administrative orders.

How to Submit a Public Comment
1
Sign in or create a MiCOURT account. MiCOURT is the electronic filing system used across the state — by prosecutors, attorneys, and members of the public alike.
2
Once signed in, go to Electronic Filing and select ADM Comment as the filing type.
3
Complete the prompts — including the ADM number you are commenting on — and submit your comment.
A staff member will review your submission. Provided the comment is not frivolous, it will typically be added to the public record within a few days. The Court is legally required to add your comment before the proposal is formally decided. Anonymous comments are accepted.

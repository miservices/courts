District Court judge application

Apply for judicial service on the Genesee County District Court. Applications are anonymous and scored on a 100-point scale. You need at least 80 points to qualify for further consideration.

Before you begin

This application is anonymous. It asks only for your State Bar of Michigan number. It never asks for your name or other identifying information, and your identity is not considered when your application is scored. Please leave your name, employer, school, county, and contact details out of every answer.

The State Bar of Michigan administers applications. Plan for about two hours. Your answers are not saved until you submit, so keep this page open until you finish.

Part 1: Multiple choice 50 points

Ten questions, five points each. Choose the best answer for each.

Part 2: Short answer 20 points

Two questions, ten points each. Write 100 to 250 words for each. Reviewers score your answers with a standard rubric.

Part 3: Written opinion drafting exercise 20 points

Read the case below and write a short opinion deciding it. Write 250 to 1,000 words.

Hollis v. Marlow Properties

Dana Hollis rented an apartment from Marlow Properties and paid a $900 security deposit. Ms. Hollis moved out on March 31. On April 3, she emailed Marlow's office manager her new mailing address. The office manager did not reply.

On May 15, Marlow mailed an itemized list to Ms. Hollis's old apartment address. The list claimed $450 for carpet stains and offered to refund the remaining $450. Ms. Hollis never received it. She sued in small claims for the full $900.

At the hearing, Marlow produced dated photographs showing stains on the living room carpet. Marlow's manager testified that the email was never seen and that the company's lease says all notices must be sent by regular mail. Ms. Hollis testified that she cleaned the apartment, that the stains were there when she moved in, and that she has no move-in photographs.

Assume this rule applies:

  • A tenant must give the landlord a forwarding address in writing within 4 days after moving out.
  • Within 30 days after the tenant moves out, the landlord must send the tenant an itemized list of any damages claimed, and any refund, to the forwarding address.
  • A landlord who misses the 30-day deadline loses the right to keep any part of the deposit.

Your opinion should state the facts, identify the issues, explain the rule, apply it to the facts, and announce a judgment.

What happens next

The State Bar of Michigan confirms that your number belongs to an attorney licensed and in good standing. Multiple choice answers and the qualifications questionnaire are scored automatically. Reviewers designated by the State Bar and the Supreme Court score the short answers and your opinion, using standard rubrics, without knowing who you are.

Applications that score at least 80 points move forward to the Supreme Court. The Court ranks them without regard to identity. After ranking, applicants' identities may be revealed, and the highest-ranked applicant is designated Chief Judge of the District Court for the next term.

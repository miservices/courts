Careers with Michigan Courts

Help keep the courts running for Flint and Genesee County. See current openings at the Genesee County District Court and the Michigan Supreme Court.

Current openings

How to apply

  1. Open a posting and read the qualifications.
  2. Select Apply and sign in to the Online Portal.
  3. Submit your resume and a short cover letter.
  4. The court contacts selected candidates to schedule interviews.

Michigan Courts is an equal opportunity employer. All qualified applicants are considered without regard to race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or veteran status.

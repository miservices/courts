Careers with Michigan Courts

Help keep the courts running for Michigan. See open positions at the Michigan Supreme Court, the State Bar, the State Court Administrative Office, the judicial and attorney oversight boards, and the Genesee County District Court.

Open positions

How to apply

  1. Open a position and read what the role involves.
  2. Select the apply button. Judges and magistrates have their own application pages. Every other role uses the general application.
  3. Submit your resume and a short cover letter.
  4. Selected candidates are contacted to schedule interviews.

Michigan Courts is an equal opportunity employer. All qualified applicants are considered without regard to race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or veteran status.

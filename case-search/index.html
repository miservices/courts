Case Search

Search public records for criminal, civil, traffic, and administrative cases in the trial courts. Looking for appeals, opinions, or orders? Use Supreme Court case search.

Tips: Type several words to narrow results, like Calloway assault. Put a phrase in quotes to match it exactly. A partial case number, like 26-D-04, works too.

About these records

These are public records from the trial courts. Cases sealed by court order are not shown. Some documents in a case may also be sealed.

For hearing dates across all cases, see the court docket. For appeals, opinions, and orders, use Supreme Court case search.

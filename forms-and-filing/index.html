How to file

The basic steps for most cases.

  1. Find the right formSearch the court forms list. If no form exists for your motion, you can prepare your own document, and the court will waive the first $25 of the fee if you ask. See the rules.
  2. Prepare your documentUse a flattened, text-searchable PDF under 5 MB, or a linked drive file. Check the page limits and signature rules.
  3. File through the Online PortalThe clerk reviews filings that start a case and tells you through the portal whether it was accepted or denied.
  4. Pay the fee or ask for a waiverFees are due within 14 days of filing. To request a waiver, submit Form MC 20. Fees and waivers.
  5. Watch for noticesAfter a case starts, you'll get a Notice of Electronic Filing for each new filing. You're responsible for checking the portal. Notice and service.

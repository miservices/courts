Court Directory

Judges, magistrates, and departments of the Genesee County District Court, and how to reach them.

Judges

The court has a Chief Judge, who is elected countywide, and associate judges, who are appointed by the Governor. Each judge is assigned a docket and a courtroom.

Magistrates and court mediator

Magistrates are appointed by the Chief Judge and handle routine matters. The court mediator helps parties in civil cases try to settle before trial.

Court staff can explain procedures, but they can't give legal advice. Most requests, like filing a document or paying a fine, can be done online through the Online Portal.

Other offices you may deal with

These offices work with the court but are not part of it:

  • Genesee County Prosecutor's Office: brings criminal cases on behalf of the State.
  • Public Defender Office: represents people who are accused of a crime and can't afford a lawyer.

To find out how to get a lawyer, see Self Help.

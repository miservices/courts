Michigan Court Online Portal

MiCOURT is the official online portal for the Michigan Court system. Sign in with your MiPASS account to access your case records, filings, and court services.

📋
Your Cases View cases you are a party to, track hearing dates, and monitor case status.
📄
E-Filing Submit documents and filings directly to the clerk's office electronically.
💳
Fines & Fees Pay outstanding court fines, fees, and financial obligations online.

What to do next

  1. Keep your Discord direct messages open. That's where summonses are sent.
  2. If you are summoned, you have 14 days from the day you receive it to acknowledge it in the juror portal.
  3. If you can't appear on the court date, request to be excused in the portal. That counts as a response, so it also acknowledges your summons. The court may excuse you for good cause.

What you're signing up for

  • Your information is added to the court's database and the voluntary juror pool.
  • When a case has a jury, jurors are chosen at random from the pool.
  • If you are summoned, you are required to appear.
  • City fire and police department employees are exempt.
  • You are paid $85.00 for every 5 minutes of service.

Online Portal

Sign in to file documents, follow your cases, and pay fines and fees without visiting the courthouse.

What you can do in the portal

Your casesSee the cases you're a party to, track hearing dates, and check each case's status.
File documentsSubmit filings to the clerk's office electronically. All filings are made through the portal right now.
Fines and feesPay court fines, costs, and other fees online.

You don't need an account to look up a case. Public case records are available through the case search and the Supreme Court case search.

Before you file

Check the filing information for file formats, page limits, and fees, and find the form you need in the court forms list. If you don't have a lawyer, start with Self Help.

Sign in

The Online Portal uses your MiGOVT account to confirm who you are. You'll go to MiGOVT to sign in, then come right back here.

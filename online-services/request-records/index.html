Request Records

Request a certified copy of a court record, a docket entry, or a court opinion from any court. Records that are sealed are not available. Certified copies are delivered as a PDF.

Fees

RequestSearch feeCertified copy
Court record$125.00$3.50 for each page
Docket entry$75.00$2.50 for each page
Court opinion$75.00$2.50 for each page. A party to the case, or the party's counsel if the party is represented, is entitled to one copy of the opinion without charge.

The fee is assessed to your account when your certified copy is issued and is added to My financials. Your copy can be downloaded for 21 days. Your receipt number lets anyone confirm the copy was issued, even after the download expires.

Court Rules

The Michigan Supreme Court adopts and maintains the rules governing practice, procedure, and professional conduct throughout the Michigan court system. All rules listed below are in effect unless otherwise noted.

Governs practice and procedure in all Michigan courts, including pleading, motion practice, discovery, trials, judgments, and appeals. Adopted and amended by the Michigan Supreme Court.

Michigan Rules of Professional Conduct

Sets the ethical obligations for attorneys licensed in Michigan, covering duties to clients, courts, and the public. Enforced by the Attorney Grievance Commission.

Local Court Rules — Genesee County District Court

Supplemental rules adopted by the Genesee County District Court governing local practice and procedure. Local rules apply in addition to the Michigan Court Rules and may not conflict with them. Adopted by the Chief Judge and approved by the State Court Administrative Office.

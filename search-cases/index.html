Case Records Access — Terms of Use

Michigan Courts Public Access System · SCAO

Purpose of This System

This public case access system is provided by the State Court Administrative Office (SCAO) to facilitate access to court records for informational purposes. Case information is derived from official court filings and may not reflect the most recent actions taken in a given case.

Terms & Conditions of Use

  • Information retrieved from this system is for informational purposes only and does not constitute an official court record or certified copy of any document.
  • You may not use information obtained from this system to harass, threaten, or harm any individual named in court records.
  • Commercial use, bulk downloading, or systematic scraping of case data is strictly prohibited without prior written authorization from SCAO.
  • All records are subject to applicable privacy laws, including MCL 600.2165 and court administrative orders.
  • Certain records — including juvenile proceedings, sealed cases, and protected party information — may be redacted or withheld from public view.
  • You acknowledge that case status information may be delayed and should not be relied upon for legal deadlines or court appearances.

Privacy Notice

Court records contain personal information including names, addresses, and other identifying details. Access to this information is governed by the Michigan Court Rules and applicable state privacy statutes. Any misuse of this information may subject you to civil or criminal liability.

⚠ Roleplay Notice

This is a fictional simulation. No real personal information is contained in this system, and no records displayed here reflect actual legal proceedings or real individuals. All case data is fabricated for demonstration purposes only.

Accuracy Disclaimer

While SCAO strives to maintain the accuracy of records in this system, the Michigan Court system makes no warranty, express or implied, regarding the completeness, accuracy, or fitness of any information for a particular purpose. Users rely on this information at their own risk. For certified copies or official record verification, contact the Clerk of the Court directly.

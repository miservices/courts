How It Works

1. Fill in the article details at the top.

2. Write your content using the editor and block inserter.

3. Click Generate Sheet Content.

4. Copy the output and paste it into column H (content) of your Google Sheet. Copy the other values into their matching columns.

5. Set status to published when ready to go live.

How It Works

1. Click a block type above to insert it into the canvas.

2. Click any text in the canvas to edit it in place — what you see is exactly what will render.

3. Hover a block to reveal ↑ ↓ ✕ controls to reorder or delete.

4. For lists, timelines, FAQs and facts, use the + Add button to add more rows/items.

5. For tables, drag column borders to resize, and use the Add/Delete row and column buttons.

6. Click Generate Sheet Content and copy into column H of your Google Sheet.

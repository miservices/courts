What happens next

  1. The Commission receives your complaint as a formal report.
  2. The commissioners review and investigate the allegation.
  3. They vote on whether the report shows misconduct. At least two of the three commissioners must agree.
  4. If they find misconduct, the Commission files with the Supreme Court, recommends a punishment, and the Grievance Administrator prosecutes the case.

Before you submit

  • The Commission looks at a judicial officer's conduct. It can't change the outcome of your case.
  • To challenge a ruling, file an appeal instead. See mandatory appeals.
  • Anonymous complaints are accepted, but the Commission can't reach you with questions.

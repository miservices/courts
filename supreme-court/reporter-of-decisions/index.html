About the Reporter of Decisions

The Reporter of Decisions is the Supreme Court office that prepares and publishes the Court's decisions. Because these decisions are binding on every Michigan court, the Reporter makes sure they are accurate, consistently formatted, and easy to find.

  • Publishes each opinion after the Court releases it.
  • Edits for accuracy, checking citations, names, and dates without changing the Court's decision.
  • Maintains the archive of every reported decision, listed below.
